# news-image-20261122

World news of 22 November 2026 summed up in one image — entry for the make.fun daily
news-image competition (slug `news-image-20261122`). The picture draws the day's most
consequential stories as a front page: COP31 climate talks running into overtime in Antalya,
three tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz, a Russian strike on Kryvyi Rih with NATO pledging
to defend Poland, the Nasdaq wiping out its 2026 gains, and a funeral boat capsizing in
Bayelsa, Nigeria. `main.py` copies the committed `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or
`/output`, standard library only, byte-identical on every run); `make_image.py` is the
generator that drew the PNG and is never run by the grader.
